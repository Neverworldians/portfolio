The Situation

The content was largely AI-generated with keywords inserted in ways that felt forced and unnatural, which hurt both search performance and credibility with visitors.

The Approach

Audience & Keyword Mapping
The venue serves two distinct audiences: corporate clients booking meetings, team offsites, and company functions, and consumers planning personal celebrations like weddings, birthdays, and milestone events. I mapped out the keyword landscape for both segments, identifying which search terms carried real booking intent and which pages should target which queries. Every page got a clear audience, a clear intent match, and a reason to exist.
Content Overhaul
I replaced the existing copy with roughly 20 new pages built around those keyword and audience maps, plus about 10 supporting blog posts designed to capture longer-tail searches and drive internal traffic to the core conversion pages. All content was written to convert, not just rank.
Paid Search
I built a media plan projecting lead volume against the available budget, presented the proposal to leadership, and got approval to launch. I ran search campaigns on phrase match targeting bottom-of-funnel venue booking queries. The first two weeks were heavy on negative keyword refinement, since Google was matching us to searches outside our target intent. Once the negative keyword list was dialed in, the campaign settled into strong performance: 4.3% CTR and $354 CPA against an average booking value of $10K–$50K.
Local Search Optimization
I fully optimized the Google Business Profile, which is one of the highest-impact, lowest-effort moves for any venue competing in local search. For a business where "near me" and map pack visibility directly drive inquiries, this was a baseline that hadn't been properly addressed.
133 leads in two years under the previous team. 127 leads in the first six months of 2026. If the current pace holds, 2026 is on track to deliver more than 3x the total output of the prior two years combined.
